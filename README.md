# 🌻 Meadowbrook Farm

A cozy isometric farming game for the browser, in the spirit of classic social farm games and Animal Crossing.
Plow and plant, raise animals, grow an orchard, craft goods in workshops, decorate with 400+ wholesome
decorations (including seasonal holiday sets), and visit your neighbors to help out and send gifts.

All of the art is drawn in code at startup, so there are no image assets to download or license.

## Features

- **Crops**: 16 crops plus 16 seasonal specials, from 1-minute strawberries to 12-hour cranberries. Crops grow in
  real time (even while you're offline) and wither if left too long. Drag across a field to plant or harvest it
  all.
- **Animals & trees**: 8 animals and 8 fruit trees that produce on a cycle. Animals blink, swish their tails and
  hop around.
- **Crafting**: 7 workshops with 30 recipes and queued production slots.
- **Barn**: everything you've harvested and made. **Sell extras** clears out surplus produce in one go, keeping ten
  of each (or more if an order needs it) for crafting and cooking.
- **Cooking mini-games** in the spirit of Cooking Mama. Chop, stir, crack eggs, whisk, knead, flip and mind the
  heat to turn farm ingredients into 15 home-cooked dishes. Cook perfectly and you make two.
- **Mama Marigold**: a jolly bear who strolls onto your farm each day to brag about her garden and bring you a
  home-cooked meal. You can visit her kitchen garden too.
- **Villager order board**: deliver produce, goods and dishes to the villagers for bonus coins and thank-you
  decorations.
- **Meadow Pond fishing**: cast, wait through the nibbles, hook on the bite and play a reel mini-game. There are
  27 fish (6 only during festivals) that change with the season and the time of day.
- **Critter catching**: butterflies, beetles and fireflies wander onto the farm; swing a net to catch one. There
  are 23 critters. The Nature Journal tracks every species and gives keepsake decorations at milestones.
- **Kitty Corner** (in the spirit of Neko Atsume): fill a food bowl and neighborhood cats drop by to nap and play
  on your goodies. Collect their gifts and pet them to make them purr and earn hearts. At 10 hearts you can adopt
  a cat, and it roams your farm. Rare cats only come for fancy food and their favorite goodie.
- **Weather**: every farm shares a daily sky with a forecast.
  - Rain and thunderstorms water crops so they grow 25% faster, and rare fish rise.
  - Wind shakes extra fruit from the trees.
  - Snow days make cooking worth more XP.
  - Rainbows add +1 to every harvest.
  - Cats shelter on your goodies when it's wet or snowy.
- **Invite links**: share your farm as a link; friends open it to stroll around and help out, no account needed.
- **Villager friendships**:
  - Chat with the six villagers and give each one gift a day. Everyone has five loved items to discover, and gifts
    on a birthday count triple. Filling a villager's order also makes them happier.
  - At 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 hearts they mail you a letter with a present, up to a unique keepsake decoration.
    Letters arrive in your farm mailbox.
- **Farmers' Market Stall**: put goods out at a bargain, fair or fancy price, and villagers buy them one at a time
  over the day. Sunny days bring more shoppers, and everyone craves something different each day, which sells for
  double.
- **Town Projects**: chip in goods and coins to improve Meadowbrook: the pond bridge, a market square, a kitty
  shelter, an orchard, a community kitchen and a bandstand. Each finished project gives your farm a lasting perk.
- **Grand Projects** (the late game, from level 15): four landmarks you raise on your own farm in four funded
  stages each, paid in coins and goods. The first stage hands you a building site to place; every stage after
  that, the landmark rises a little further out of its scaffolding. A finished landmark works its perk while it
  stands on your farm:
  - the Grand Greenhouse gives a 1-in-4 chance of a bonus crop at harvest;
  - the Lighthouse Windmill makes workshops craft 25% faster;
  - the Hot-Air Balloon Dock's balloon drops off a gift basket every day;
  - the Botanical Conservatory adds 10% to sales and orders.

  The Decor page's **Grand** shelf sells gold, pearl and crystal decorations for 100k to 1.5M coins, and a quest
  chain from level 15 to 30 walks you through it all.
- **Flower breeding**: tulips and roses planted next to their own kind sprout new flowers beside them, four times
  a day. Tap a flower with the hand to water it (rain waters them all) and it is far more likely to breed. The
  right colour pairs bloom into nine rare hybrids, from orange tulips to golden roses that only open in the rain.
  The Flower Album keeps track, with villagers' hints for the ones you haven't found, and five hybrids earn a
  Florist's Cart.
- **County Fair**: every weekend (Saturday and Sunday, UTC) Meadowbrook holds four contests: Prize Produce, a
  Bake-Off, a Flower Show and Biggest Catch. Enter one thing in each; the judge scores it on its value, their own
  taste and a bonus for the fair's featured item, against three villagers' entries. Ribbons pay prize tickets for
  the Prize Booth's fairground decorations, and a clean sweep earns the Grand Champion Trophy.
- **Crop mastery** (from the original FarmVille): harvest a crop again and again to earn up to three mastery
  stars. Each star pays coins and XP and unlocks a perk for that crop: seeds cost 10% less, then a 1-in-4 chance
  of a bumper harvest, then +1 on every harvest and a Mastery Sign keepsake. Stars show on the seed cards, in the
  crop's tooltip and on the Crop Mastery tab of the Collections panel.
- **Collections** (from the original FarmVille): little treasures sometimes turn up while you harvest and collect,
  in six sets of five (Garden Party, Golden Fields, Berry Basket, Barnyard Treasures, Orchard Keepsakes and
  Afternoon Tea). Turn a full set in for coins, XP and a mystery gift, plus a keepsake decoration the first time.
  The villagers will swap three spares for a piece you're missing.
- **Lonely animals** (from the original FarmVille): from level 3, a lost little animal with a name wanders onto
  your farm once a day. Adopt it (it settles in nearby with its name, as a baby) or walk it to the villager who has
  been hoping for one, for coins, XP, a mystery gift and friendship. It only rests on open grass, so it never
  stands in the way of a harvest.
- **Baby animals and the Nursery Barn** (from FarmVille 2): the Market's Animals tab has a Babies shelf where a
  chick, calf, lamb, piglet, duckling, kid or bunny kit costs half price. Babies are drawn small and make nothing
  until they grow up (three production cycles, from 30 minutes up to 12 hours). Tap one for a cuddle once an hour to
  skip a tenth of the wait; neighbors can feed them too. A Nursery Barn makes every cuddle and treat within three
  tiles count double, and tapping it lists every little one with a "cuddle everyone" button.
- **Feed Mill** (from FarmVille 2): a level 3 workshop that grinds wheat and carrots into Barnyard Feed (and corn
  and blueberries into Deluxe Feed). Tap a busy animal to feed it: it skips a quarter of its wait (Deluxe: half) and
  gives +1 (Deluxe: +2) at its next collection. Babies can snack as often as you like and grow up sooner with each
  bite. The mill's panel has a "feed hungry animals" button.
- **Vehicles** (from the original FarmVille): the toolbar's Garage sells a Tractor (plows a 2×2 patch), a Seeder
  (plants your chosen seeds in a 2×2 patch) and a Harvester (harvests a 3×3 patch). Pick one and tap the farm: the
  patch lights up as you aim (red when there's nothing to do there) and the whole patch is worked in one go, with
  every bonus you'd get by hand. Each drive uses one unit from a shared 12-unit fuel tank that refills one unit
  every 10 minutes. Dragging still pans the farm while you drive.
- **Helpers** (from the original FarmVille): a Farmhand collects every ready animal, an Arborist picks every ready
  tree and an Unwither Charm brings every withered crop back, ripe. You can't buy them with coins: they come from
  quests (Many Hands, A Little Fairy Magic, Helping Out), now and then inside a mystery gift, with every completed
  collection, and from the County Fair's Prize Booth for tickets. Use them from the Garage, whose button shows a
  badge when one has work waiting.
- **Workshop upgrades** (from FarmVille 2): every workshop's panel has an Upgrades section. Level 2 (coins plus
  5 of the workshop's first good) adds a queue slot and crafts 15% faster; level 3 (more coins, 10 of its first
  good and 5 of its second) adds another slot, crafts 30% faster and gives a 1-in-4 chance of a bonus item with
  every job. Upgraded workshops show their stars in the tooltip.
- **Well water** (from FarmVille 2): pick up the Water tool and tap or drag over growing crops to give them a 25%
  head start, once per crop. The can holds 10 splashes and slowly trickles back by itself, or tap a Wishing Well or
  Water Pump to fill it to the brim (each well refills every two hours). On rainy days the sky does the watering.
- **Mystery gifts**: surprise presents that drop while you farm and unwrap into decorations.
- **Over 400 decorations** in six collections:
  - *core*: flowers, plants, fences, paths, furniture, lights, water and whimsy
  - *holiday lifestyle*
  - *Gardening Mama-style horticulture*
  - *Halloween Spooktacular*
  - *Countryside nostalgia*: red barn, tractor, treehouse and a playground
  - *Festivals & parties*

  Windmills spin, flames flicker, fountains splash, ghosts float and trees sway. A **Decor Catalog** tracks
  everything you've collected.
- **The Decor page** (the pink Decor button next to the Market, or the lantern in the corner on phones) holds
  every decoration:
  - It has a category rail, search (by name, category or festival), sorting, and *Can afford* and *New to me*
    filters.
  - The Seasonal shelf includes each festival's token shop.
  - A Rewards shelf shows the decorations you earn rather than buy, and how to earn each one.
  - A Storage tab holds everything the Store tool has tucked away.
  - The page remembers where you were, so you can place something and pop straight back.
  - `node scripts/decor-check.mjs <screenshot-dir>` tests it.
- **15 seasonal events**: Lunar New Year, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, Cherry Blossom Viewing, the
  Spring Egg Hunt, Earth Day, the Summer Festival, the Star Festival, the Mid-Autumn Moon Festival, the Harvest
  Festival, Halloween, the Festival of Lights, Thanksgiving, the Winter Holidays and New Year. Each one has:
  - its own **currency** that drops while you farm, and a **token shop** of exclusives
  - an event crop and costumed **visitors** (trick-or-treaters, carolers, lion dancers, egg hunters and more)
  - animals in **costumes**
  - a quest chain
  - a playlist of **three or four songs**, holiday touches on the UI sounds (sleigh bells, firecrackers, a
    bubbling cauldron, birdsong, a gobble...), particles, lighting and a title screen
- **Villagers**: Mayor Maple, Henrietta, Penny, Rosie, Grandma Fern and Mama Marigold, each with a painted
  portrait, quests and a themed farm you can visit and help.
- **Ribbons**: FarmVille-style yellow, white, red and blue ribbons for lifetime milestones.
- **Coziness**:
  - A **music box of 66 songs**, all synthesized (no audio files). Outside festivals the farm's own songs follow
    the clock and the sky: morning, day, evening, night, rain and snow. Every festival has its own playlist,
    with public-domain tunes arranged for their holidays (Jingle Bells, Deck the Halls, Greensleeves, Sakura,
    Auld Lang Syne...) next to new ones. Instruments include a music box, koto, erhu, tin whistle, sitar, steel
    pan and theremin, with drums to match: taiko, bodhrán, tabla and sleigh bells. Songs are written in
    `client/src/music/tracks.ts` and composed and arranged by `client/src/music/compose.ts`. Each song plays a
    few times through, arranged a bit differently each time, then rests and gives way to another.
  - Day/night lighting from your local clock, with glowing lamps and fireflies.
  - Seasonal weather and grass, drifting clouds and butterflies, and a puppy and kitten who wander your farm.
  - Coins that fly to your HUD, and farm postcards you can save.
- **Progression**: 50 levels, land expansions from 14×14 up to 30×30, a daily bonus and a charm rating.
- **Neighbors**: visit friends' farms and fertilize, revive, water or feed them (5 helps per neighbor per day),
  send daily gifts, and read the farm-news feed.
- **Server-authoritative**: the same rules run on the client for instant feedback and on the server to validate
  them, with seeded randomness so both sides always agree.

## Tech

| Package   | What                                                                                |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `shared/` | Game content and rules (a pure reducer), used by both client and server             |
| `server/` | Express 5 + Node's built-in SQLite (`node:sqlite`): accounts, farms, neighbors, gifts |
| `client/` | Phaser 3 + Vite + TypeScript, with a DOM overlay for the UI                         |

All art, including the HUD icons, is drawn in code. The interface kit (jelly buttons, quilted panels, ribbon
titles, leafy tabs, the mint and sunshine palette) takes its look from
[Animal Island UI](https://github.com/guokaigdg/animal-island-ui) (MIT), restyled in plain CSS.
Run `node scripts/ui-tour.mjs <dir> [WxH]` to screenshot the whole kit at any screen size, and
`node scripts/first-play.mjs <dir> [WxH]` to play the opening minutes of a brand-new farm.

To check pacing, `npx tsx scripts/balance-sim.ts [days] [casual|keen]` plays a simulated farmer through the real
rules (four or twelve visits a day) and reports when each level arrives, where the coins come from and which quests
stall. With `SAVE=<path>` it also writes each simulated farm as a browser-only save (`<path>-<profile>.json`), which
the next two scripts load:

- `node scripts/phone-tour.mjs <save.json> <dir> [WxH] [url]` opens that farm on a phone-sized browser and
  screenshots every main screen (farm, menu, quests, orders, barn, decor, neighbors and so on).
- `npx tsx scripts/perf-check.ts <dir> <save.json>... [--throttle=4] [--url=...]` measures frame rate, update and
  render time on each farm, plus one with every free tile decorated, on a CPU slowed to phone speed. Headless
  Chromium draws with a software GPU, so compare runs with each other rather than trusting the absolute fps.

Requires **Node 22.13+**. Node's built-in SQLite prints an "experimental" warning, which is harmless.

## Getting started

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # server on :3001, client on http://localhost:5173
```

Open http://localhost:5173, create a farmer, and start farming. To try the neighbor features, register a
second farmer in a private window and send a neighbor request.

### Production

```bash
npm run build        # builds client/dist
npm start            # serves the API and the built client on http://localhost:3001
```

### GitHub Pages (no server)

The game also ships as a browser-only build that needs no server. The farm is saved on the player's device
(localStorage), and the shared rules and the villagers' farms run in the page. Neighbors are the villagers: you can
visit, help and send gifts to them. The Farmhouse has **💾 Back up** and **📂 Restore** buttons that move a farm
between devices as a file.

```bash
npm run dev:local -w client   # play the browser-only build at http://localhost:5173
npm run build:pages           # builds client/dist-pages (relative paths, so any static host works)
```

**Invite links** let friends see each other's farms without accounts. **🔗 Share link** (in Neighbors or the Farmhouse) makes a
link that holds a compressed copy of your farm after the `#`, so no server ever sees it. A villager-sized farm comes out
at about 900 characters, small enough for a chat message. Opening a link takes you straight to that farm. A brand-new
player starts their own farm first. In the browser-only build the friend is saved in Neighbors, where you can help them
five times a day, and a newer link from the same friend replaces the old copy. In the server build a link is a
look-only visit. `node scripts/invite-check.mjs <screenshot-dir>` plays a share-and-visit round trip.

`.github/workflows/pages.yml` tests, builds and deploys this build on every push to `main`. It can also be run
by hand from the Actions tab. You only need to set it up once: in the repository, go to **Settings → Pages →
Build and deployment** and set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**. `node scripts/pages-check.mjs <screenshot-dir>
[url] [WxH]` play-tests a built copy. By default it expects the copy to be served at
`http://localhost:8099/webfarmville/`.

Environment variables:

| Variable          | Default        | Purpose                                                       |
| ----------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PORT`            | `3001`         | HTTP port                                                     |
| `FARM_DB`         | `data/farm.db` | SQLite database file                                          |
| `FARM_ALL_EVENTS` | unset          | Set to `1` to unlock every seasonal shop (handy for testing)  |

### Checks

```bash
npm test             # game-rule and API tests (vitest)
npm run typecheck    # TypeScript across all packages
```

  `horticulture`, `spooky_a`/`spooky_b`, `country_a`/`country_b`, `festivals_a`/`festivals_b`, `halloween`,
  `events_a` through `events_e`, `cooking`, `portraits`, `pets`, `fx` and the newer feature modules (`mastery`,
  `collections`, `nursery`, ...). You can also view
  it live at http://localhost:5173/art-preview.html?mod=all.
- `node scripts/swipe-check.mjs <screenshot-dir> [url]` checks touch farming on the browser-only build with real
  multi-touch swipes: harvest and seed strokes, two-finger panning and the hand.
- `node scripts/smoke.mjs <screenshot-dir> <url>` drives a real browser through a play session. Companion
  scripts check other features (`scripts/blooms-check.mjs` and `scripts/collections-check.mjs` run with `npx tsx`;

## Controls

- The **Hand** taps things to harvest, collect or open them, and drags to look around.
- The **🧺 Harvest** basket is for sweeping: swipe or drag anywhere across the farm and everything ripe along the way
  is gathered (crops, animals, trees, finished crafts and withered plots). The **Seeds** bag works the same way
  for planting. A stroke never moves the camera, fast swipes are traced tile by tile so nothing is skipped, and
  the pops climb a little scale as you go. The seed bag only plows new ground when a stroke starts on grass, so
  swiping across a field never plows the lawn around it.
- Use the **toolbar** to switch between Hand, Harvest, Plow, Seeds, Water, Market, Move, Store and Sell, or open
  the Garage to drive a vehicle. When things are ready, tap the "✨ N ready" hint to pick up the basket.
- On touch screens, **two fingers** move the camera with any tool, and pinching zooms. With a mouse, right-drag
  pans with any tool. **Scroll** zooms, and **WASD** or the arrow keys also pan.
- **R** flips a decoration while placing or moving it. **Esc** or right-click cancels.
- **Phones** get their own layout: a dock with Hand, Harvest, Seeds, Water and Market (the other tools wait behind
  **More**), Quests, Orders and Barn on the side with everything else in the **Farm Menu**, and panels that slide
  up as sheets (swipe one down to close it). While a tool is busy, the hint above the dock has **Flip** and
  **Done** buttons in place of R and Esc. **Tablets** keep the full toolbar with bigger, labelled buttons.
- The game can be **added to a home screen** and opens full-screen like an app (the Farm Menu has the button, or
  the how-to on iPhones). A service worker keeps the game files on the device. The app icons are painted from the
  game's own art by `node scripts/app-icons.mjs`.
- Add `?hour=22` to the URL to preview night time (or any hour of the day), and `?event=winter` (or any event id)
  to preview an event's look, music and visitors any time of year (`?event=none` turns festivals off). `?music=`
  pins a playlist or a song by id (`?music=rain`, `?music=jingle_bells`). `node scripts/music-check.mjs
  <screenshot-dir>` renders every song offline to check the mix, then checks which playlist plays when.

## Adding content

Game mechanics can live in their own feature module. Add a file under `shared/src/features/` that calls
`registerFeature` with its actions, and use module augmentation on `ExtraActions` and `ExtraEvents` for its
types. Then add a matching `client/src/features/*.ts` with `init(ctx)` for HUD buttons, panels and scene
effects. The reducer passes each feature the same coin, XP and item helpers that it uses itself.

Content lives in `shared/src/content/`. Add a crop, animal, building, recipe, decoration or quest there, then
paint its art in the matching `client/src/art/*.ts` module. `client/src/art/manifest.ts` lists the texture keys
each module must provide. Anything missing shows up as a placeholder crate in the game and as "Missing" on the
art contact sheet.
